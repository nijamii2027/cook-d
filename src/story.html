Food we genuinely want to eat.

Cook'd is a neighbourhood café built around food we genuinely want to eat. Our menu takes inspiration from across Asia, shaped by Western techniques and a lot of curiosity.

We start with what makes a dish good, keep its character, then play around the edges—a familiar breakfast reworked through a different technique, or a drink combination that sounds slightly questionable until you try it.

Our menu moves with the seasons as we test new ideas and keep changing things when we think we can make them better. Thoughtful food that doesn't take itself too seriously.
