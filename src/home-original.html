About us

Familiar, with a few unexpected turns.

Food we genuinely want to eat.

Cook'd is a neighbourhood café built around food we genuinely want to eat. Our menu takes inspiration from across Asia, shaped by Western techniques and a lot of curiosity.

We start with what makes a dish good, keep its character, then play around the edges—a familiar breakfast reworked through a different technique, or a drink combination that sounds slightly questionable until you try it.

Our menu moves with the seasons as we test new ideas and keep changing things when we think we can make them better. Thoughtful food that doesn't take itself too seriously.

From root to cup

Serious coffee. No complication.

Our coffee is roasted by Five Senses Coffee, with espresso and milk-based classics alongside a rotating selection of drinks developed in-house.

Beyond coffee, we play with flavours we love—tea, matcha, hojicha, fruit and other familiar ingredients brought together in slightly different ways.

Meet Bagel

Head of unclear responsibilities.

The face hiding around Cook'd.

Inspired by our real-life dog, Bagel is curious, slightly lazy and usually more interested in what's happening in the kitchen than actually helping.

You'll spot him throughout Cook'd in illustrations, packaging and the occasional unexpected place. His job description is unclear. He seems comfortable with that.
